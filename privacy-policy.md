# Privacy Policy for Prism Ring

**App:** Prism Ring: Color Wheel Game  
**iOS Bundle ID / Android Package:** `com.prismbloom.game`  
**Platforms:** iOS (iPhone & iPad) and Android  
**Developer:** brianoksoftware  
**Effective date:** 30 October 2026

This policy explains what Prism Ring does with information when you play the app on iOS (iPhone or iPad) or Android. The developer does not run an account system and does not operate a backend server for this game.

## Information stored on your device

The app saves the following locally on your device, and only on your device:

- Your best score and best combo
- Whether sound and haptics are turned on
- Whether ads have been turned off on that device

This information is stored using standard on-device storage (`UserDefaults` / `AsyncStorage`) and is never transmitted to the developer. Uninstalling the app deletes it. There is no account, username, email, or online leaderboard.

## Advertising (Non-Personalized)

The app displays family-safe (`G`-rated) ads through Google AdMob. Ads may appear as a banner on the main menu, an occasional full-screen interstitial ad after completed runs, and an optional rewarded ad if you choose to revive a run.

All ad requests are made as **non-personalized** (`requestNonPersonalizedAdsOnly: true`). We do not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission on iOS and do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

To deliver, measure, cap frequency, and protect against fraud, Google AdMob may automatically receive standard technical information from your device, such as:

- IP address (which may be used by Google to estimate coarse/general region)
- General device and OS information (model, screen size, language)
- Ad performance and interaction events (whether an ad was loaded, shown, or tapped) and Apple `SKAdNetwork` attribution signals on iOS
- Device advertising or vendor identifiers where permitted by your operating system settings

That information is processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can manage privacy and advertising controls at any time in **iOS Settings > Privacy & Security** or **Android Settings > Google > Ads**.

## Permissions

The app uses network access to load ads and plays audio for in-game sound effects and music. It does **not** request or use your microphone, camera, contacts, photo library, health data, or precise GPS location.

## What this app does not do

- It does not ask you to create an account or sign in
- It does not track you across third-party apps or websites
- It does not sell or rent your personal information
- It does not include any first-party analytics or tracking server operated by the developer

## Children's Privacy

Prism Ring is a general-audience arcade puzzle game (`4+` on the Apple App Store) and only serves family-safe (`MaxAdContentRating.G`), non-personalized advertisements. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
