Privacy & Support

Prism Ring: Color Wheel Game

Bundle ID / Package: com.prismbloom.game • Platforms: iOS (iPhone & iPad) & Android • Effective Date: 30 October 2026

This policy explains how Prism Ring: Color Wheel Game handles information when you play on iOS (iPhone or iPad) or Android. The developer (brianoksoftware) does not run an account system and does not operate a backend server for this game.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

The app saves the following locally on your device, and only on your device:

  • Your best score and best combo streak
  • Whether sound effects/music and haptics are turned on
  • Whether ads have been removed on that device

This data is stored using standard on-device storage (UserDefaults / AsyncStorage) and is never transmitted to the developer. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

2. Advertising (Non-Personalized)

The app displays family-safe (G-rated) ads through Google AdMob. Ads may appear as a banner on the main menu, an occasional full-screen interstitial ad after completed runs, and an optional rewarded ad if you choose to revive a run.

All ad requests are made as non-personalized (requestNonPersonalizedAdsOnly: true). We do not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission on iOS and do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

To deliver ads, cap frequency, and prevent fraud, Google AdMob may automatically receive standard technical information from your device (such as coarse IP region, device model/screen size, ad interaction events, and Apple SKAdNetwork attribution signals) governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Permissions

Prism Ring uses network access to load ads and plays audio for in-game sound effects and music. It does not request or use your microphone, camera, contacts, photo library, health data, or precise GPS location.

4. Children's Privacy

Prism Ring is a general-audience arcade puzzle game (4+ on the Apple App Store) and only serves family-safe (MaxAdContentRating.G), non-personalized advertisements. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
